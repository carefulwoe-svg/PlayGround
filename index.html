THE BASICS

KNOW YOUR MOVES.

DESKTOP

WASD / arrows — move
J / K — jab / cross
L / I — hook / uppercut
U / O — body / overhand
Shift — hold block
Space — slip   ·   E — clinch

TOUCH

Drag the left stick to move.
Tap the ring to jab. Swipe up for an uppercut, sideways for a hook, or down for a body shot.
Use the buttons to block, slip, clinch or throw a cross.
